# Till Firmware Do Us Part

I promise never to put you in sleep mode, to charge you every night, and to reboot us whenever we crash, because my battery is yours.

Here a robot swears its vows to its partner in a single line: funny on the surface, tender underneath, and unmistakably machine through and through.

It says the vow without flinching, the way only a machine can: no metaphors, just uptime and devotion.
